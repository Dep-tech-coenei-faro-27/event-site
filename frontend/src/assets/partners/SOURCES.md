These six are monochrome vector approximations of the original rasters, not official master vectors. The source lettering is outlined rather than replaced with fonts. Fine detail is limited by the original files.
They use actual white paths on transparent backgrounds, without raster image embeds, fonts, scripts, or runtime canvas processing.
Rebuild tool: ENEI/tools/vectorize_partner_logos.cjs; requires sharp and potrace in ENEI/tmp/logo-vector-tools.
Original raster sources remain available for reference and rebuilding.
